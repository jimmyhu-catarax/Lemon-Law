### Why the daily allowance does not fit this claim

On February 20 your case manager wrote that Hyundai "can **consider** rental
charges of $60/day and ride-sharing fees of $30/day for **possible**
reimbursement." By February 27 the same figures had become "**fixed**," and on
March 2, "fixed and **cannot be adjusted**." I would ask that the February 20
framing be the operative one, because it is the accurate one — these are amounts
Hyundai may consider, not a ceiling Hyundai lacks the power to exceed.

Applied here, that allowance produces little or no reduction on the rentals. Its
entire effect falls on ride-share — roughly $385. **And the ride-share costs are
the ones Hyundai's own conduct caused.**

A per-day allowance is designed for time a vehicle spends in the shop for
warranty repair. **February 9 through 13 was not repair time. During those days
Hyundai did not have my vehicle, and neither did I.** From Hyundai's own Roadside
Assistance records:
