## Email body

Hi Mike,

Attached is Exhibit 11 — the Days Out of Service Summary the June 3 index
referred to. As I mentioned, it was never actually attached to any of the
submission emails, so the arbitrator never received it and it has no record
status.

Because it was drafted back in early May, before I had scrubbed my numbers,
it contains several errors I've since corrected. Since you're drafting now, I
want you to have them up front rather than build on the wrong figures:

1. **It states the statutory threshold as "15 days" (citing §198-a(b)(2))
   and computes "13.4× the minimum" from that.** The out-of-service
   presumption threshold is **30 days** — that is how the arbitration
   decision form itself frames the issue ("out of service due to repairs for
   30 or more days"). All of the document's multiplier math is built on the
   wrong number.
2. **It describes the February 2026 repair (RO #370267) as a "second HV
   battery replacement."** The repair order shows a **12-volt** battery
   replaced under warranty. There was one high-voltage pack replacement, in
   2025 (RO #358530). This is the same mischaracterization that appears in
   my served Exhibits 12 and 22 and the Loss of Use Record — flagged on the
   record-bundle cover page (Flag 8).
3. **It gives the lease date as May 22, 2024.** The lease agreement says
   **May 23, 2024**. (The arbitration form has the same one-day slip.)
4. **It leads with 201 total days (198 "conservative").** The number I would
   stand behind is the single-repair-order figure: **124 days** on RO
   #358530 alone (April 3 – August 5, 2025, "VEH. DISABLED," odometer
   advanced 3 miles) — Hyundai's own document, no aggregation needed. The
   176-day from-first-report figure in its first row is consistent with the
   repair orders; the 15-day and 7-day 2026 periods rest partly on roadside
   records rather than dealer ROs.
5. **It says "Filing Date: May 4, 2026."** The AG file date in NYSDRA's
   letter is May 12, 2026 (fee paid May 11).

One thing in it that is accurate and may be useful: it documents that two of
Paramus's repair orders (#358530 and #363888) list a customer email address
that is not mine — "jimmy.hu25@gmail.com" — while the other two ROs have it
right. That error originated in Hyundai's records and fits the documented
breakdown in their communications with me.

Happy to walk through any of it.
