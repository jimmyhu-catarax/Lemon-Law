SUMMARY OF EVIDENCE 
 
 
Consumer: 
Consumer purchased new 2024 WAGON ION from Hyundai Brooklyn (currently in his 
possession under a lease extension renewed on a month to month basis as he can 
have it available for inspection if needed). Consumer alleges the vehicle is a lemon as 
it has been out of service for over 200 days and he did not have access to a loaner the 
whole (part of the time) and has approx. 1,800 in expenses associated w having to 
take cabs etc to work he is a physician. He is concerned about lost time dealing with 
the dealership who operated in bad faith. He states he brought the car to Paramus NJ 
workshop for battery draining issue, even when fully charged, he states this is a 
manufacturing defect and the total days out of service qualify the vehicle as a lemon. 
He reviewed his experience being stranded in Feb 2026 and the car did not start, the 
car was towed and the roadside assistance was able to jumpstart the car. He is 
frustrated with the two Buy Back offers from the manufacturer and did not accept those 
not due to the $ amount but was concerned about a clause related to the state of CA 
that he did not think applied. Subsequently, he had a lot of correspondence with the 
dealer and provided extensive exhibits. He summarized the days out of service as 
listed in his form but all factors are taken in totality. Arbitation provided full opportunity 
for consumer to cover timelines, RO and his position that the car is a lemon. Based on 
testimony that the vehicle is currently operating and no allegation that the use and 
value is impaired, the consumer has not met his burden of proof. 
 
 
Manufacturer: 
 
Counsel for manufacturer indicates the consumer testified the vehicle is in his 
possession and currently driving as intended so the case should be dismissed.Detailed 
review of ROs, and industry standard is for technician to duplicate customer concern 
and diagnostics performed. There was a first voluntary recall on TSV RO 356398 
2/12/25 7,026 mileage and software update performed and multi part inspected 
completed. RO 258530 4/3/2025 mileage 8,148, concern of battery draining too quickly 
even when fully charged. The repair needed back order parts which is a challenge in 
whole industry. This RO not related to recall repairs, took time but loaner provided no 
charge. RO 363888 mileage 8662 2/18/2026 car not starting, customer is stranded, 
again battery replaced and NWP. Note electric vehicles batteries need to be replaced 
no guarantee by any manufacturer of a defect free car. Here the vehicle is operating at 
intended and still under warranty the consumer refused two buy back offers made, 
multiple attempts to contact his attorney. The dealership wants to make customer 
whole, however this car can not be deemed a lemon as the problem does not still exist 
and there were not four attempts to repair the same problem. Consumer has extended 
the lease and seeks reimbursement of 1800 in expenses when car out of service, 
second time he did not receive a loaner which is on a first come first serve basis. 
Unrelated to this lemon law arbitration. The manufacturer's argument is compelling.

ARBITRATOR'S FINDINGS (Check all that apply. Only one of the findings is necessary for 
a ruling in favor of the manufacturer.) 
 
1. The vehicle is not primarily used for personal, family or household purposes. 
 
2. There were not 4 or more repair attempts for the same problem within 18,000 
miles or 24 months, whichever is earlier. 
 
3. The vehicle was not out of service due to repairs for 30 or more days within 
18,000 miles or 24 months, whichever is earlier. 
 
4. The problem did not continue to exist at the end of the 4th repair attempt. 
 
5. The problem did not substantially impair the value of the vehicle to the consumer. 
 
6. The manufacturer did not fail to commence repairs within 20 days of receiving 
the consumer's notice of the dealer's refusal. 
 
7. The problem is a result of the consumer's abuse, neglect or unauthorized 
modification or alteration of the vehicle. 
 
8. Other:   
CONCLUSION 
Based on the findings, I find that the consumer does not qualify for a refund or a replacement 
vehicle under General Business Law '198-a (The Lemon Law). The decision is not intended to limit 
the consumer's rights under the manufacturer's warranty or the manufacturer's duty to repair under 
the Lemon Law (GBL, Sect. 198 -a(B)). This decision is binding on both parties.  However, a 
dissatisfied party, pursuant to CPLR Article 75, may seek judicial relief, by filing an application in 
court within 90 days of receiving this award.
