IV. STRATEGIC SIGNIFICANCE — WHY THIS DOCUMENT IS CRITICAL
This Smart Diagnosis printout establishes four key facts:
    ●    The battery/charging system (VCMS) and chassis battery voltage (ABSC/ESC) failures are recorded in entirely separate
         ECU modules from the right headlamp faults (ICU). These are distinct electronic systems. A broken headlamp assembly
         cannot cause a VCMS battery charger fault.
    ●    Hyundai's own diagnostic tool — not Consumer's testimony — generated these codes. This is Hyundai's own equipment
         identifying a powertrain/battery failure.
    ●    The headlamp codes (8235300, 8258A00) are consistent with the pre-existing cosmetic damage present since December
         22, 2024, which was not flagged when the vehicle was returned to Consumer on August 7, 2025 after the first 126-day
         repair event.
    ●    The February 19, 2026 warranty confirmation (RO #370267) — which authorized battery replacement at no charge —
         directly refutes any claim that the headlamp caused the battery failures.


In summary: Hyundai's own Smart Diagnosis system shows P1BAD92 and C110213 as battery/powertrain system faults on
January 8, 2026. Six weeks later, Hyundai's own technicians confirmed those same faults were warranty-covered manufacturing
defects requiring battery replacement. The headlamp was irrelevant.
