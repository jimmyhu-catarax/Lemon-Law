III. NARRATIVE STATEMENT
On April 3, 2025, Consumer brought the subject vehicle (2024 Hyundai IONIQ 5, VIN KM8KNDDF9RU253237) to Paramus
Hyundai Service and Parts after discovering the high-voltage battery had degraded to a 37-mile estimated range at 100%
charge. The dealership retained the vehicle for warranty repair and issued a loaner vehicle — a Hyundai Elantra bearing New
Jersey license plate G20 HUL — at no cost to Consumer.
Consumer drove this loaner vehicle from on or about April 3, 2025 through August 7, 2025, a period of approximately 126
consecutive days, while the IONIQ 5 remained at the service center awaiting backordered battery parts. During this entire
period, Consumer received no timeline for repair completion from Hyundai.
The Hyundai Elantra loaner is a compact sedan significantly smaller than the leased IONIQ 5 SUV. Consumer is a practicing
ophthalmologic surgeon with a demanding schedule requiring reliable, comfortable, and safe transportation to and between
medical practice locations. The low-seated, compact profile of the Elantra caused Consumer back and neck pain throughout
the loaner period, creating significant physical discomfort and professional distress during a demanding surgical schedule.
Multiple calls to Hyundai corporate customer service during this period (HMA Case #410-472-20, opened June 16, 2025)
yielded no response or resolution.
