     NYSDRA does this through its statewide partnership network of Community Dispute Resolution Centers (CDRCs) that
     service all 62 counties in New York State.

     In addition to providing arbitration under the Lemon Law Arbitration Program, CDRCs provide a wide range of conflict
     resolution options including mediation, arbitration, conflict coaching, restorative justice circles, school interventions, and
     many other services. These services are designed to provide the timely and satisfactory resolution of a wide range of
     conflicts in areas such as court-related matters, family, youth, disability services, property, and a host of other disputes. In
     the majority of cases, parties resolve their issues in a manner that leads to improved relationships, better communication,
