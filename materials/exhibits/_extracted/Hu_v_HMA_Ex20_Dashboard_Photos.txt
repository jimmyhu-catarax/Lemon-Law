Photo 1 — Feb 19, 2025 (7,223 mi) — FIRST DOCUMENTED DEGRADATION: 127 mi at 91% Charge
[CRITICAL]
 CRITICAL EVIDENCE. Only 127 miles at 91% charge. At delivery (May 2024), 90% charge yielded approximately 270
 miles — this represents greater than 50% loss in functional range, and is the first documented manifestation of the
 battery defect. Consumer first became aware of the defect at this date. The Hyundai Smart Diagnosis report (Exhibit
 10) documents the underlying fault codes.




Photo 2 — Apr 1, 2025 (8,087 mi) — Accelerating Failure: Only 17 mi at 41% Charge




 Two days before Consumer drove the vehicle to Paramus Hyundai on April 3. Only 17 miles at 41% charge — a
 functioning IONIQ 5 at 41% charge would show approximately 110+ miles of range. The vehicle was becoming
 completely unusable for any meaningful travel. This photograph corroborates the urgency of Consumer's decision to
 bring the vehicle directly to Paramus Hyundai on April 3, 2025.




Photo 3 — Apr 3, 2025 (8,128 mi) — PIVOTAL: Only 37 mi at 100% Full Charge — Day Consumer
Drove to Paramus and Left Vehicle for Service [PIVOTAL]
 THE KEY PHOTOGRAPH IN THIS EXHIBIT. Dashboard shows only 37 miles at 100% charge with Level 2 charging
 confirmed complete. A fully charged 2024 Hyundai IONIQ 5 SEL AWD should display approximately 266+ miles at
 100% charge. This photograph directly corroborates Repair Order #358530 (Exhibit 4), in which Paramus Hyundai
 confirmed 'deviations in multiple cells' via Techline diagnostics. Consumer drove the vehicle to the service center on
 this date, left it with Paramus Hyundai, and received a loaner vehicle. The vehicle remained at the service center for
 126 consecutive days. Odometer reads 8,128 miles — consistent with RO documentation.




Photo 4 — Aug 12, 2025 (8,276 mi) — 'Refill Coolant' Warning: 5 Days After Pickup
  Consumer retrieved the vehicle on August 7, 2025 after 126 consecutive days in service. Just 5 days later — August
  12, 2025 — the dashboard displayed a prominent 'Refill coolant' warning with orange alert icon. The vehicle was
  immediately returned to Paramus Hyundai for service completed August 28–29, 2025. Odometer reads 8,276 — only
  148 miles driven in 5 days post-pickup. Combined with the April–August repair period, total documented loss-of-use
  reaches 179+ days.
