## The screening question that separates candidates

Nearly every lemon-law firm's model is high-volume *pre-arbitration* demand/
settlement practice. Hu needs the opposite posture: a **post-award CPLR
7511(b)(1)(iii) special proceeding** against an adverse NYAG-program award,
on the broad compulsory-arbitration review standard. No firm found advertises
that specific posture. Ask each candidate first:

> "Have you filed an Article 75 petition to vacate an adverse NY AG-program
> lemon-law arbitration award? In which county and department, and what was
> the outcome?"

Also disclose the **September 9, 2026** filing deadline in the first contact.
