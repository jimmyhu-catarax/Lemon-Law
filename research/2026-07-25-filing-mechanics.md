## 2. VENUE — New York County is proper

CPLR 7502(a)(i): where the arbitration agreement names no county, proceedings
affecting arbitration other than to stay or bar it are brought in the county
where **at least one of the parties resides or is doing business**, or where the
arbitration was held or is pending.

Petitioner's address of record — on the award itself and on the NYSDRA Decision
Letter — is **320 West 38th Street, Apt. 2427, New York, NY 10018**, which is in
**New York County**. Venue is proper on the face of the petition. New York
County Supreme Court sits in the **First Department**, which is where the
*Leonidou* and *Riina* vacaturs come from — the favorable-department posture the
memo relies on. (Confidence: high. The statutory text is search-sourced;
Petitioner's address is verified from the award and Decision Letter.)

## 3. COMMENCEMENT AND THE RETURN DATE

A special proceeding is commenced by **filing** (CPLR 304), not by service.

| Step | Requirement | Source / confidence |
|---|---|---|
| File | Notice of Petition + Verified Petition (+ exhibits, + memorandum of law) with the New York County Clerk; index number issues on payment | High |
| Index number fee | **$210** | Search-sourced; confirm at the counter |
| RJI | Required to get the case before a Justice; fee **$95**; file the RJI with the papers | Search-sourced |
| Return date | Petitioner selects it. Papers should be **filed with the Clerk at least 5 business days before the return date** so the matter appears on the court's calendar | Search-sourced (NY County Supreme, self-represented guidance) |
| Service deadline | CPLR 403(b): notice of petition + petition served on the adverse party **at least 8 days before** the return date. If served **at least 12 days before**, the notice may **demand** an answer at least 7 days before, with reply 1 day before | High |
| **Jurisdictional bite** | Late service defeats jurisdiction and requires dismissal on timely objection, regardless of prejudice | Search-sourced — treat as hard |
| Verification | CPLR 402 requires the petition be **verified**. Do not file it unverified | High |
| E-filing | NYSCEF is available; whether it is mandatory or consensual for a pro se Article 75 special proceeding in NY County must be confirmed at filing | **Unresolved — ask the Clerk** |

**Practical calendar.** Build in slack; the 8-day and 12-day figures are floors,
not targets. Working backward from the locked **September 9, 2026** deadline:

- File (and thereby commence) **no later than early August 2026**. Filing, not
  service, stops the 90-day clock — but do not test that at the margin.
- Pick a return date **≥ 20 days after intended service**, and serve using the
  **12-day** track so the notice can demand an answer 7 days out. That gives
  time to reply and avoids an adjournment fight.
- **Do not let the filing slip into September.** A single failed service attempt
  on a foreign corporation eats a week.

## 4. SERVICE ON HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA — the one open variable

CPLR 403(c): the notice of petition is served **in the same manner as a
summons**. For a corporation that means CPLR 311(a)(1) — personal delivery to an
officer, director, managing or general agent, cashier, assistant cashier, or an
agent authorized by appointment or by law — or the Business Corporation Law
routes:

- **BCL § 306** — if HMA **is** authorized to do business in New York, the
  Secretary of State is its statutory agent; serve two copies at the Department
  of State in Albany with the fee. This is the ordinary, cheapest, cleanest route.
- **BCL § 307** — if HMA is **not** authorized, service on the Secretary of
  State is permitted, **but § 307 layers on extra steps** (personal delivery at
  the Department of State in Albany plus a follow-up mailing to the corporation
  and a filed affidavit of compliance). § 307 is technical and is a classic
  source of defective-service dismissals. Do not improvise it.

**ACTION ITEM (user, before service — 10 minutes):** run HMA on the NY
Department of State Corporation and Business Entity Search
(dos.ny.gov → Corporation and Business Entity Search Database), or call the
Division of Corporations at (518) 473-2492. Record:

1. Whether "Hyundai Motor America" appears as an **authorized foreign
   corporation** in New York, and its DOS ID;
2. Its **state of incorporation** (the petition currently brackets this);
3. The **"Service of Process" / DOS process address** on file;
4. Any **registered agent** (Hyundai Capital America — a different entity — uses
   National Registered Agents, Inc.; do **not** assume HMA is the same).

Then serve via BCL § 306 if authorized. **Strong recommendation: use a licensed
process server**, not personal effort. The affidavit of service is the document
the court checks first, and a pro se petitioner may not serve his own papers.

Note the address NYSDRA used for HMA in this arbitration — **1122 Cranbury South
River Rd., Jamesburg, NJ 08831** — is a regional office. It is *not* a substitute
for statutory service, though a courtesy copy there (and to the HMA case
representatives already on the e-mail record) is sensible.

# UPDATE — 2026-07-28: CPLR 306-b, and the real service deadline

**This section corrects an omission in § 4 above.** The original memo covered
*how* to serve HMA but never *by when*. That is governed by **CPLR 306-b**, and
for this proceeding it is much tighter than the ordinary rule.

## The rule

The 120-day service window most practitioners think of does **not** apply here.
CPLR 306-b provides that where the applicable statute of limitations is **four
months or less**, service must be made **not later than fifteen days after the
date on which the applicable statute of limitations expires**. (Confirmed
across four independent search renderings quoting the statutory text, including
Justia's codification and two practitioner treatments. **Direct fetch still
403s — confirm against McKinney's before relying on it in court.**)

Article 75's period here is **90 days** — under four months — so the short rule
governs.

## What that means on this calendar

| Event | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Award delivered | June 11, 2026 | Decision Letter |
| **Limitations period expires (file by)** | **September 9, 2026** | CPLR 7511(a) — locked |
| **Service on HMA must be complete by** | **September 24, 2026** | CPLR 306-b (15 days after expiry) |

**The service date is fixed to the expiry of the limitations period, not to the
filing date.** Filing early does not shorten it; filing late does not extend
it. Two consequences:

1. **Filing in mid-August buys real slack.** File ~August 17 and there are
   roughly five weeks to complete service on a foreign corporation before the
   September 24 wall — enough to survive one failed attempt, a wrong process
   address, or a BCL § 307 mailing sequence.
2. **Filing on September 8 does not.** The service wall stays at September 24
   no matter how late the petition goes in, which compresses everything —
   process server, DOS route, affidavit of service — into about two weeks.

If service is not made in time, 306-b directs dismissal **without prejudice**
on motion, but permits an extension "upon good cause shown or in the interest
of justice." Do not plan around that safety net: it is discretionary, and
here a dismissal without prejudice is functionally with prejudice, because the
90 days will have run and cannot be refiled.

## Revised recommended calendar
